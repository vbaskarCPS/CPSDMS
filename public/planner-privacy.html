Planner: privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Planner is a private planner for one household. It is not offered to the public and nobody outside the household can sign in. This page says what happens when one of us connects a Gmail mailbox to it.

What connecting Gmail lets the planner do

It can

  • Read the messages in the mailbox you connected, so the planner’s assistant can show them, sum them up when you ask, and offer to-dos from what comes in. Offering to-dos is a switch on each mailbox: it starts on, and you can turn it off in Settings › Email. While it is on, the planner looks at new email in the main inbox about every half hour.
  • Send an email from that mailbox, only after you have been shown who it goes to, the subject and the full text, and have tapped Confirm.

It cannot

  • Delete or move email, mark it as read, or change any Gmail setting. It does not ask Google for permission to do those things.
  • See your Google password. You sign in on Google’s own page.

What is kept, and where

  • The address of each mailbox you connect, and the key Google gives the planner for it. The key is stored encrypted on the planner’s server and is never sent to the app on your phone or computer.
  • To-dos offered from an email: what to do, who it is from, the subject and when it arrived.
  • What the assistant told you about an email, and any email you asked it to write, as part of your conversation with it.
  • If something goes wrong, a short note of the problem, so it can be fixed.

Your email itself stays in Gmail. The planner reads a message when it is needed and does not keep a copy of your mailbox. Card numbers, passwords, verification codes and ID numbers that appear in an email are not copied into to-dos or notes.

Who else handles it

  • To sum up an email or pick out a to-do, its text is sent to Anthropic’s Claude, the AI service behind the assistant, which returns the answer. Anthropic’s terms for this service say it does not use what is sent this way to train its models.
  • The planner’s data is stored with Supabase, and the site is served by Vercel.

Nothing from your mailbox is sold, used for advertising, or passed to anyone else. Nobody reads your email through the planner, including the person who runs it, unless you ask for help with a particular message.

The planner uses what it receives from Google only as described on this page, and follows Google’s API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

In the planner, Settings › Email › Disconnect removes the mailbox, deletes its stored key and asks Google to cancel the access. You can also remove the planner from Google’s side at myaccount.google.com/connections. To-dos that were already offered or added stay in your planner until you remove them.
